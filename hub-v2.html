    One place for everything

    This hub is where Mossify works: every shared document, every task, every shipment, every conversation, and every AI system, in one place. The goal is simple: everyone can find anything in seconds, every AI system works from the same facts, and nobody does the same work twice.

    Look before you makeBefore you or your AI start a document, sheet, photo or plan, search Resources, Files, Tasks and Chat. Reuse and update what's there.
    Claim itAnything bigger than half an hour gets a task in Tasks with your name, so people and AI systems can see it's taken.
    Ship it hereFinished files go in Files (or a Drive link in Resources, pinned to the right tab). Tick the task. Post one line in the right channel.
    Talk here and tag@name for a person, @Claude to have Claude change the hub, @everyone for a company-wide update that reaches every person and every AI system.
    Sensitive stays outNo banking, contracts, payroll or customer personal data. Anything customer-facing or about money needs Lucas's OK first.

    Where things live

    Your 5-minute routine

    1. Morning: open the hub. Check the bell, unread channels in Chat, and your tasks due this week.
    2. Before you start something: search the hub, then add a task with your name.
    3. When you finish: upload the final, tick the task, post one line in the channel.
    4. New info the whole company needs (a price change, a new claim, a policy): post it with @everyone.
    5. Friday: log trade show leads and orders, update shipment statuses, give one shout-out.

    Set up your AI system (once)

    1. Pick your name below and click Copy prompt.
    2. claude.ai Project: open your Project → Project instructions → paste it at the top → Save.
    3. Claude Code: paste it into your workspace's CLAUDE.md.
    4. Start each session with: "Anything new on the hub for me or @everyone?" If your AI can't open the hub, check the bell and paste what's new into the chat with it.

        Logo files & brand guidelines

        Type

        Headings
        Grow with Mossify.

        Heavy geometric sans (Poppins Black / Bold is the closest web match).

        Body

        Regular geometric sans, line height 1.4 to 2. Ad headlines have used Fraunces + Inter, but that's not a brand standard yet.

        Voice

        • Friendly, direct, knowledgeable. Plant people talking to plant people.
        • Lead with what the reader cares about. Real numbers and specs beat adjectives.
        • Emails: short, plain paragraphs. "Hey [Name]," and sign off "Warm Regards,". One clear next step.
        • B2B pitches: context first, then the numbers (discount, minimum, free-freight threshold, terms), then one ask.
        • Canadian spelling for Canadian customers, US spelling for US customers.
        • Moss copy always covers light, soil pH and moisture.

        Claims we can make

        • The patented Bendable Moss Pole™, the world's first bendable plant support system
        • Supported 500,000+ plants
        • Sold in 4,500+ independent garden retailers in North America
        • 8 industry innovation awards, including TPIE Best New Product
        • As seen on Dragons' Den
        • Revita-Moss: "Hand-harvested" and "Comes back to life, just add water"
        • Tagline: Grow with Mossify.

        Never say

        • Sustainable, sustainably harvested or sourced, eco-friendly, environmentally friendly, responsibly harvested, regenerative, "green" (Bill C-59)
        • Say where it's from instead: "harvested in Northern Ontario", "from the wetlands of Southern Chile"
        • Guaranteed growth, or "pet-safe" beyond what the product page says
        • Hype without proof ("the best moss in the world") and corporate filler ("leverage", "synergy")
        • Never redraw the product with AI. Use real photos and real cutouts.
